English - Grammar Notes

1. Sentence structure: Subject + Verb + Object.
2. Common parts of speech:
   - Noun
   - Pronoun
   - Verb
   - Adjective
   - Adverb
   - Preposition
   - Conjunction
3. Example:
   The student (noun) reads (verb) a book (object).
4. Practice:
   - Identify the nouns and verbs in each sentence.
   - Rewrite the sentence using an adverb.
